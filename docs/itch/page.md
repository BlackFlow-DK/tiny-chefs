## Short description (the "Short description or tagline" field)
Tiny chefs, giant food. Co-op kitchen chaos for 2 to 4 friends on the same network.

## Description (the big text box)

**You are one metre tall. The tomato is not.**

Tiny Chefs is a co-op cooking game for 2 to 4 players on the same Wi-Fi or LAN. Everything in the kitchen is
enormous, so you carry ingredients together, chop them, fry them, stack the plate and ring the bell before
the customer gives up.

- Four kitchens: the Diner, a Food Truck that lurches, a windy Picnic table and the Twin Islands with one
  narrow plank between them
- 15 dishes, from a plain burger to The Works
- Campaign with stars, endless mode and custom shifts
- Earn coins, buy upgrades between shifts: boxing gloves, faster cooking, a second plate and more
- Dress your chef: hats, beards, outfits, back items and body shapes

**How to play together**
1. Everyone downloads the game for their computer (Windows or Mac) and joins the same network.
2. One player clicks Host.
3. Everyone else clicks Join and picks that kitchen from the list. If the list stays empty, type the
   address the host's lobby shows instead.

Everyone needs the same version of the game. Some school and guest Wi-Fi networks stop computers from
seeing each other: if nobody shows up, connect everyone to one phone hotspot.

**Controls**

| | Keyboard and mouse |
|---|---|
| Move | WASD or arrow keys |
| Aim | Mouse pointer |
| Grab / let go | Left click or E |
| Chop, pour (hold) | Right click or F |
| Punch | Space or Q |
| Ping a spot | Middle click or R |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel, or two-finger scroll on a trackpad |
| Pause | Esc |

**Windows: first start**
Unzip and run TinyChefs.exe. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.
When you host for the first time, allow the game through the firewall on private networks.

**Mac: first start**
The game is not signed with a paid Apple developer account, so your Mac asks you to approve it once.
- Easiest: install the free itch.io app (https://itch.io/app) and install Tiny Chefs from there.
- Or, after downloading in a browser: unzip, move Tiny Chefs to Applications and open it. When the Mac says
  it could not verify the app, click Done, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
  Open Anyway. On macOS 14 or older: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open.
- When you host or join, the Mac asks to let Tiny Chefs find devices on your local network: click Allow.

Needs macOS 13 or newer on Apple Silicon Macs (macOS 11 on Intel Macs).
